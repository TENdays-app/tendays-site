Source register

Every statement the app makes about safety, food, licensing or the law — the document it rests on, and when a person last opened that document and confirmed it still says so.

    How to use this register

    Each hazard block shows what the app says — its hazard line, advice and every control — and below it the sources it cites. For each source, open the link, read the relevant part, and check the app’s lines against it. If they agree, tick the box on that source’s row: the database records who ticked it and when, and the next check falls due twelve months later. If the source says something different, open Notes, link and history, write what you found and press Doesn’t match — that flags the claim purple for a rewrite. A link marked unconfirmed is a best guess until a check confirms it or you save a better one. Nothing recorded here can be edited or deleted afterwards, and Export CSV gives the whole register as a spreadsheet — that is the evidence.
