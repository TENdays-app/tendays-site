Get the app See how it works

Ten minutes of questions. Then a plan.

No blank page, no spreadsheet that drifts. Everything hangs off the date of your event.

1

Describe the event

What it is, where, when, how many people, what's happening: alcohol, live music, a BBQ, fireworks, a road to close.

2

Get your deadlines

Every task is counted back from the day. The TEN deadline is worked out in clear working days, bank holidays included, and shown green, amber or red.

3

Confirm your risk assessment

Hazards and controls built for your activities. Confirm each one; the ones that need doing become tasks, the ones for the day go in the briefing.

4

Share it with the committee

Give people areas to look after, assign tasks, talk in the app, and brief your volunteers on the day. The lead keeps the overview.

5

Download the pack

A council-ready pack: risk assessment, event plan, TEN crib sheet and finance summary, from one place.

6

Do it again next year

Repeat an event and carry your committee, learnings and ticket types forward. Dates and deadlines start fresh.

What it covers

The parts of a community event that are easy to get wrong and hard to look up.

  • Temporary Event Notices. Whether you need one, the standard and late deadlines, the 499-person limit, and a crib sheet for the form.
  • Risk assessment. Hazard sections matched to your activities, from crowd numbers to BBQ fuel, with a sign-off your committee stands behind.
  • Road closures. When to apply for a temporary traffic order, tracked as its own deadline.
  • Numbers that matter. First aiders, stewards, toilets and bins suggested from your attendance, ready to adjust.
  • Committee and volunteers. Roles, areas of responsibility, tasks with owners, and a day-of briefing volunteers can read on their phones.
  • Money. Ticket sales, other income and costs against each task, with a net figure and a spreadsheet export for the treasurer.
  • On the day. A checklist that unlocks as the day approaches, so nothing is left in someone's head.
  • The debrief. Record what happened and what you'd change, then repeat the event next year with the lessons attached.

A straight word

TENdays is a planning tool, not a licensing or safety adviser. You stay the author of record for your event's paperwork. TENdays helps you make it complete, on time, and easy to share with the people who need to see it.
