Privacy

What TENdays collects, why, who sees it, and what you can do about it.

This is an interim notice while TENdays is in testing. The full privacy policy is being finalised before public launch and will replace this page. Nothing here will get less protective.

Last updated: 7 September 2026. Contact: hello@tendays.app

What we collect

When you create an accountYour name, your email address, and a phone number if you add one (it pre-fills official paperwork such as the TEN crib sheet).
When you plan an eventEvent details (name, type, dates, postcode, expected attendance, activities); the committee members you invite and the roles and areas you give them; tasks and who they are assigned to; risk-assessment answers and sign-offs; figures you enter for costs, tickets and income; messages, photos and files you send in the app; and whether you hold a personal licence, if you tell us.
AutomaticallyThe technical data needed to run the service: a sign-in session on your device, a push-notification token if you allow notifications, and basic request logs held by our hosting provider.

We do not collect payment details. We do not use advertising or tracking.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service you asked for. Building your plan, sharing it with your committee, sending sign-in codes and the reminders the plan itself generates.
  • To keep the service safe. A bot check on sign-in, rate limits, and logs to investigate faults or misuse.
  • Only with your consent: occasional emails about TENdays, and asking how your event went. Both are opt-in switches in Settings and can be turned off any time. Reminders about your own event are part of the service, not marketing.

Who sees it

  • Other people in your event. Committee members see what their role allows. Day-of volunteers see the briefing, anything they are named for, and one message channel.
  • Providers that run TENdays for us, each under a contract to process data only on our instructions: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Resend (sends our emails), Cloudflare (the bot check on sign-in), Postcodes.io (turns a postcode into a licensing authority), and Expo with Apple and Google (delivering the app and push notifications).

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, and while an event you were part of is still in use by its committee.
  • When you delete your account, your personal details are removed within 30 days. Events stay available to the rest of their committee, and what you did within them stays in the event's record without your name. See Delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email hello@tendays.app and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

TENdays is for adults organising events and is not intended for children. Where a committee records details about young volunteers, the committee is responsible for handling them lawfully.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change, and update the date above. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
